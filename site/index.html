More about the data (for grown-ups) · تفاصيل للكبار
What each label means. observed (ADS-B) seen in the air by free community trackers (adsb.fi, adsb.lol), with the route from adsbdb.com (hexdb.io when adsbdb has none); departure time is estimated from distance flown. (OpenSky is tried daily but refuses anonymous access.) observed (airport board) the airport's own board reports it departed (TLV: Israel Airports Authority open data; MED: Tibah Airports public board). scheduled published but not yet confirmed as departed (timetable, not observed). Seats are estimates based on aircraft type, and are only known for some flights. Real passenger numbers aren't public. Coverage is limited: JED and AMM have no airport-board data. Free trackers have very few receivers inside Saudi Arabia, so many JED/MED flights are missed until they leave Saudi airspace. Codeshares are counted once unless you include them. dlat/dlon = destination minus origin (airport or mosque); km = great-circle distance; "mod 27" = value mod 27 (positive remainder); modes use the whole-number part. Prayer windows: Makkah and Madinah use Umm al-Qura; Al-Aqsa uses the Jordan Ministry of Awqaf method, computed at the mosque, for both TLV and AMM. Last recited: the surah and ayat of each mosque's latest audible prayer (Fajr, Maghrib, Isha), identified from the YouTube recording of that prayer (data/recitations.json, else data/last_recited.json; updated daily). Shown only if at most 3 days old. Dhuhr and Asr are silent prayers, so they have no ayat. Number-pattern surah: used only when a mosque has no recent recitation data. It is NOT the live recitation (no public feed says what is being recited). Formula: take that site's flights for its local today in the current prayer window (if fewer than 3, the whole day; if still fewer than 3, the last 7 days); a = most common whole-number dlat mod 27 and k = most common whole-number km mod 27, both measured from the mosque (ties: smallest value); surah = ((27 × a + k) mod 114) + 1, which is always 1 to 114.
