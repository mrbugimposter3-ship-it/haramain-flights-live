What each label means. observed (ADS-B) seen in the air by free community trackers (adsb.fi, adsb.lol, OpenSky), with the route from adsbdb.com; departure time is estimated unless OpenSky reports it. observed (airport board) the airport's own board reports it departed (TLV: Israel Airports Authority open data; MED: Tibah Airports public board). scheduled published but not yet confirmed as departed (timetable, not observed). Seats are estimates based on aircraft type, and are only known for some flights. Real passenger numbers aren't public. Coverage is limited: JED and AMM have no airport-board data. Free trackers have very few receivers inside Saudi Arabia, so many JED/MED flights are missed until they leave Saudi airspace. Codeshares are counted once unless you include them. dlat/dlon = destination minus origin (airport or mosque); km = great-circle distance; "mod 27" = value mod 27 (positive remainder); modes use the whole-number part. Prayer windows: Makkah and Madinah use Umm al-Qura; Al-Aqsa uses the Jordan Ministry of Awqaf method, computed at the mosque, for both TLV and AMM.
